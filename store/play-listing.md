## Short description (max 80)

## Full description (max 4000)

As a parent of three, I wanted one simple thing: let my kids enjoy videos without worrying about what comes next.

The problem with regular video apps is everything I didn't choose: random recommendations, videos that weren't meant for children, and a search box that lets kids go anywhere. Kids' video apps solve part of that, but they often leave out content my kids enjoyed, especially gaming channels that are fun without being violent or vulgar. I found myself switching between apps all day, and that's not how a family should watch TV.

Hassle Free TV is my answer.

HOW IT WORKS
• A parent signs in with Google on their phone or computer at tv.ehfservices.com
• Add whole channels you trust, playlists (including your own that grow over time), or single videos
• Open Hassle Free TV on your Android TV or Google TV, and link it with a short code shown on screen
• Your kids see only what's on your list

FOR PARENTS
• No search box and no surprise recommendations on the TV
• Block words such as "horror" or "GTA": matching videos are hidden automatically
• Hide Shorts and very short clips
• Daily screen-time limit that works across every device in the family
• See what was watched this week, and for how long
• Everything is protected by a 4-digit parent PIN
• Change channels from your own phone; the TV updates by itself

MADE FOR THE TV REMOTE
• Big, easy cards you move between with the arrow keys
• OK to play or pause, left and right to skip 10 seconds, Back to choose another video
• Kids can't open other websites from the app

PRIVACY
Children don't create accounts and we don't collect personal information from children. The parent's name and email are used only to save their settings. Watch history is kept for 7 days and can be cleared anytime. No ads.
Privacy policy: https://tv.ehfservices.com/privacy

I built it for my own family. I hope it makes screen time a little more hassle-free for yours too.
— Sultan Wasif, founder of Hassle Free Services

## App access (instructions for reviewers)
> The TV app is linked to a parent account with a one-time code.
> 1. Open Hassle Free TV on the TV. It shows a 6-character code (e.g. K7P-4QX).
> 2. On a phone or computer, open https://tv.ehfservices.com/tv and sign in with any Google account.
> 3. Create a 4-digit parent PIN (any digits), then type the code from the TV and press "Link TV".
> 4. To show videos: on the phone, tap ⚙️ → enter the PIN → under "Approved channels" add `@LEGO` → tap Save.
>    The TV shows the videos within a few seconds (or press ⚙️ on the TV → Reload videos).
> Remote: arrows to move, OK to play/pause, Back to close the video.

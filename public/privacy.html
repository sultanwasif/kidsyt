Kids Tube — Privacy Policy

Operated by Hassle Free Services (ehfservices.com). Last updated: [DATE]

Kids Tube is a tool for parents. A parent signs in with Google and chooses which YouTube channels their children may watch. Children do not create accounts and we do not collect personal information from children.

What we collect

  • From the parent's Google sign-in: name, email address, profile picture and a Google account identifier. We do not request access to your YouTube account, contacts, or any other Google data.
  • Settings you enter: approved channels, playlists and videos, blocked words, screen-time limit, and your parent PIN (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Screen-time totals: number of seconds watched per day, so the daily limit works across devices.
  • Watch history: which videos were played on your account (title, channel, thumbnail) and for how many minutes each day, so you can review it in Parent settings. It is kept for 7 days and then deleted automatically, and you can clear it at any time. It is linked only to the parent's account; we do not collect who in the family watched.
  • Technical data: a sign-in cookie to keep you logged in, and standard server logs (IP address, time of request) kept for security.

How we use it

Only to run Kids Tube: to save your settings, show videos from your approved channels, and enforce the time limit. We do not sell data, show our own ads, or share data with third parties except the service providers needed to run the app.

YouTube

Kids Tube uses YouTube API Services. Videos are played in YouTube's embedded player, and YouTube may collect data as described in the Google Privacy Policy. By using Kids Tube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. You can revoke Kids Tube's access to your Google account at any time from your Google security settings.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Parent settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your settings, PIN, screen-time totals and watch history. Otherwise, watch history is deleted after 7 days and daily screen-time totals after 60 days.
